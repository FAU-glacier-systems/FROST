# Findings (2026-10-22)

Run central_europe_esmda_velyear: tau_ref inverted on the geometry of the velocity year (ITS_LIVE 2014-2022 composite, 2018), forward runs from the 2000 geometry, ES-MDA with 32 members and 4 iterations, absolute-sill dh/dt covariance, model error 0.5 m/yr. 24 of 25 glaciers (Gietro crashed in the grid coarsening; fixed, rerun pending).

- Bed and data: no artefacts. Bed roughness on the ice 0.2-1.1 times that of the ice-free terrain, 0-7 single-cell pits per glacier. NASADEM and COPDEM align (0-cell shift); on the ice COPDEM - NASADEM matches Hugonnet dh/dt x 12.5 yr (r 0.73-0.95) with +5-8 m left (radar penetration of NASADEM), so the thickness moved back to 2000 is consistent. Thickness of 2000 against GlaThiDa: Rhone +2 m, Aletsch -10 m, Pasterze +50 m, Plaine Morte +51 m, Claridenfirn -42 m. The outline cells carry 25-77 m of ice (cliffs at the margins).
- tau_ref: no sign of overfitting (< 1.5 % of the ice at the bounds, small-scale correlation with the observed speed -0.37 to 0.25).
- Velocity in time: the forward speed in 2018 is within 25 % of the inverted one on 18 of 24 glaciers, but the speeds of 2000 are 1.3-6.5 times those of 2018. Most likely an error of the ice-flow emulator, not of the data: inversion and forward runs use the pretrained network only (never retrained, no physics solve), which matches 2018 by construction but extrapolates on the thicker 2000 geometry (Pasterze lower third 24x faster in 2000 than in 2018, a shallow-ice estimate gives 1.7x; Aletsch upper third 6.6x against 1.2x; Rhone 1.4x against 1.3x). Test pending: experiments/emulator_test (frozen, retrained, solver).
- Jamtal: ITS_LIVE is noise there (p99 on the ice 1.8 m/yr); tau_ref fitted to it makes the ice immobile, it piles up on steep slopes and the emulator bursts to the 1000 m/yr cap. Not usable. Now: velocities only where their p99 on the ice exceeds 3 m/yr (min_velocity_p99), else the default tau_ref (Jamtal, Careser, Vedretta Lunga, Adler, Basodino, Malevalle).
- dh/dt: glacier means within 0.25 m/yr of Hugonnet; chi2/n against the data error alone 3-4 on Aletsch, Rhone, Findel, Kesselwand; flux converges at mid elevations and too little reaches the tongues.
- References: the GLAMOS / WGMS ELA is capped at the glacier top in years without accumulation area (Plaine Morte 80 % of the years, Careser 75, Tsanfleuron 70, Vedretta Lunga 69, Gries 60, Basodino 55, Jamtal 50, Silvretta 35). Their mean ELA is a lower bound; the ELA scores now leave these 8 out (ELA r 0.88, bias +39 m, MAE 94 m on 15 glaciers). The SMB profiles against the mean mass balance per elevation bin avoid the definitions: RMSE 0.87 m w.e./yr, 87 % of the bins inside +-2 std (submitted run 1.07, 75 %).
- Without ice flow (experiments/central_europe_noflow, dh/dt = smb with melt-out): on the 16 slow glaciers (ITS_LIVE p99 < 10 m/yr) the SMB profiles are as good as with IGM (RMSE 0.77 against 0.84 m w.e./yr), on the fast ones clearly worse (1.64 against 0.94).

Next: the emulator test; then rerun with retraining (if it removes the start-up shock) and the velocity threshold; ice flow only where it matters (fast glaciers), no flow on the slow ones.
